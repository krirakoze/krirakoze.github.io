PROJECT 01 / AUTOMATION

A finance report that knows when the bills arrive.

From structured transactions to projected balances, with calculation tools and human review before the final email.

CIS 515 · November 2026Documented coursework + offline reconstruction
Request project materials ↗

Christa Irakoze · CIS 515: AI & Data Analytics Strategy · November 2026

The problem

Monthly totals can hide a cash shortage between payments. A useful finance report must know the current balance, which transactions have already cleared, and the exact order of future income and bills.

What I built in the course

My Week 3 assignment documents an n8n workflow that retrieves transactions and account balances from Supabase/Postgres, merges both inputs, passes them to an AI Agent with a Calculator tool, and sends a report through Gmail. Human feedback supports review and revision before the final report.

An early edge case showed why dates alone were insufficient: income and rent fell on November 30. Adding transaction times allowed the workflow to put the 8 AM paycheck before the 5 PM rent payment. The assignment reports a projected ending balance of $650 after this correction.

This public deliverable

The demonstration uses fictional records and deterministic arithmetic to make the calculation auditable. It does not claim to be the original n8n export. See workflow.md (available on request) for the integration design and schema.sql (available on request) for a demo Postgres schema.

Outputs: cash-flow chart, report (available on request), ordered projection (available on request), and an edge-case verification file. A cleared income record contributes to month-to-date income but is not added again to the opening balance.

Design decisions

  • Use exact timestamps, amount, status, and account, not model guesses.
  • Treat the balance snapshot as authoritative as of its timestamp.
  • Reject ambiguous equal timestamps rather than invent a sequence.
  • Compute a minimum-balance shortfall as max(0, minimum_required - lowest_projected_balance).
  • The AI writes an explanation; arithmetic and factual records remain inspectable.

Limits and attribution

The sample assumes scheduled bills happen as recorded, uses one checking account and USD, and does not forecast unrecorded spending. Human approval and Gmail delivery are documented course features, not executed by this offline demo. The schema here is a new sanitized reconstruction, not the recovered database schema. No bank integration is claimed.

Evidence: Christa_Irakoze_CIS515_Week3_Workflow_Assignment(5).docx, November 4, 2026, sections 1–6. Recovered source says $2,800 recorded income, $1,650 checking balance, and $650 projected balance. The fictional sample uses those stated values to illustrate the logic.

Portfolio card

Built a database-grounded finance reporting workflow using n8n, Supabase/Postgres, calculation tools, Gmail, and human review. Resolved a same-day payment-order ambiguity by adding transaction timestamps and made projected balances traceable to source records.
